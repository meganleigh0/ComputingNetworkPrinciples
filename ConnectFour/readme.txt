25 Business Intelligence Questions for Claude AI Agent

Objective: Develop a conversational AI agent connected to Gardner White’s SQL data warehouse that can accurately answer business questions involving sales, store performance, customers, products, inventory, and executive-level analytics.

Sales & Revenue

* What were total net sales across all stores last week?
* How do last week’s net sales compare to the same week last year?
* Which five stores generated the highest net sales last month?
* What were total net sales by product category last quarter?
* What was our average order value over the past 30 days?

Store Performance

* Which stores experienced the largest year-over-year sales declines last month?
* What is the sales performance of each store compared to its assigned target?
* Which stores have the highest and lowest average order values?
* How does same-store sales growth compare to total company sales growth?
* Which stores had declining sales despite increasing customer traffic?

Customer Analytics

* How many unique customers made a purchase last month?
* What percentage of customers were new versus returning in the last 90 days?
* What is the average order value for new customers compared to returning customers?
* Which customer segments generated the most revenue last quarter?
* What percentage of customers who purchased last year have made another purchase this year?

Product & Inventory Analytics

* Which ten furniture products generated the most revenue in the last 90 days?
* Which product categories experienced the largest year-over-year revenue decline?
* Which products have the highest inventory value but the lowest recent sales velocity?
* How many active products are currently out of stock?
* Which product categories have the highest gross profit margins?

Executive & Advanced Analytics

* What were the three largest contributors to the company’s year-over-year sales change last month?
* Which stores are underperforming relative to the company average across sales growth, traffic, and conversion?
* How much of our revenue growth this year came from e-commerce versus physical stores?
* Which product categories have declining sales despite increasing inventory levels?
* What are the five most significant measurable performance changes this week compared to last week?

Recommendation: Use these 25 questions as your initial agent evaluation framework, progressing from basic SQL aggregations to joins, time-series comparisons, and multi-step analytical reasoning.

Before implementation, verify that the necessary data exists in your warehouse and establish approved definitions for metrics such as net sales, new customers, and same-store growth.

For each question, I would document the expected SQL query, verified answer, execution time, and agent accuracy. This will give you a measurable way to demonstrate the agent’s capabilities to leadership.
